Frequently Answered

Everything you need to know about partnering.

Clear answers to common questions about onboarding, timelines, and financial security.

How fast can Future Kart onboard our brand? +
Most brands are fully onboarded and connected to our live operations desk within 5 to 7 business days, including catalog sync and reconciliation setup.
Which platforms and marketplaces do you support? +
We support all major marketplaces (Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra, Nykaa), Quick Commerce platforms (Blinkit, Zepto, Swiggy Instamart, BigBasket), and custom D2C stores (Shopify, WooCommerce).
How does the payment reconciliation process work? +
We ingest your order data and match every individual transaction against bank remittances, marketplace commission reports, return rates, and shipping charges to identify claims and claw back lost revenue.
Do you handle international / cross-border stores? +
Yes. We support Indian brands expanding overseas (e.g. GCC, Europe, USA, Russia) as well as international brands establishing distribution within India.
